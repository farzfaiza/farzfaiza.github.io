How my thinking applies to UX research

Behavioral data can show what people did. It rarely shows why. I choose methods by asking which question each one can answer: analytics and observation for behavior, interviews and think-aloud sessions for reasoning, surveys and ratings for attitudes. A good research plan says where each method stops being useful.
